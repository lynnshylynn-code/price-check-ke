Kenyan supermarket public listing snapshot
Collection date: 20 October 2026 (Africa/Nairobi, UTC+03:00)

File: kenya_supermarket_public_listings.csv
239 unique supermarket/product listings: Naivas 176, Carrefour 25, Quickmart 38.
This is a partial public catalogue snapshot, not an exhaustive catalogue, sales dataset, or historical price series.

Columns
supermarket: retailer publishing the listing.
item_name: advertised name, with original wording retained.
price_kes: advertised selling price in Kenyan shillings; numeric decimal.
pack_size: explicit listing size where available, otherwise extracted literally from the item name. Multipacks and per-kg/per-bunch price bases are preserved where identifiable. Appliance capacities are not treated as pack sizes.
units_sold: empty for every row; no sales volumes were publicly available.
currency: KES.
regular_price_kes: crossed-out comparison price when present, otherwise empty.
product_url: public product URL, without tracking/offer query parameters.
listing_url: public listing page where the record was collected.
location_context: store context when verified; blank means unknown. Carrefour displayed Westlands - Nairobi. Quickmart store view 7 is marked separately because its branch name was not verified.
collected_at: collection timestamp with UTC+03:00 offset. For saved initial pages, this records processing time within the same collection session.
collection_method: direct_public_html or rendered_public_html.
pack_size_source: explicit_listing_field or item_name; blank when no clear size.
data_notes: missing-size or other record-specific notes.

Quality and collection notes
- All 239 rows have non-empty item names, product URLs, and positive numeric prices.
- Duplicates were removed using supermarket and canonical product URL.
- All units_sold cells are empty, not zero and not inferred from a shopping-cart quantity control.
- 26 pack_size cells are empty rather than guessed.
- Crossed-out prices are kept separate from the current advertised price.
- Product-name spelling and retailer size descriptions have not been silently corrected.
- Size text is not normalized to grams or millilitres. It may describe a physical pack, multipack, or loose-item price basis.
- Catalogue completeness, stock availability, checkout price, and eligibility for promotions have not been verified.
- Prices can vary by store, location, session, and promotion.
- Quickmart anonymous category links redirected to the homepage. Those repeated offers were deduplicated; additional public listings were collected from rendered homepage/store views.
- Carrefour direct requests timed out. Its rows were collected from product cards rendered in a browser, across dairy/eggs, food cupboard, and beverages.
- Naivas rows were collected from the homepage and publicly linked category/offer pages. One beverage category URL returned 404 and contributed no rows.
- This file contains factual public listing observations. No separate dataset licence or permission for a particular model-training use was obtained or asserted.
