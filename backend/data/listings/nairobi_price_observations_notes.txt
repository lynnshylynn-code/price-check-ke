Nairobi public-listing snapshot
Checked 16 November 2026, approximately 21:33–22:26 EAT.

The CSV stores the product-page listing price, retailer product URL, visible
location context, and collection time. The retailers do not expose a reliable
price-observation timestamp, so observed_at records when the public page was
checked. Stock and checkout totals were not verified.

Naivas pages displayed “Deliver to Nairobi, Kenya”. This verifies a Nairobi
delivery destination only; it does not identify a serving branch or prove
coverage of Nairobi neighbourhoods.

Quickmart product pages used for groceries explicitly identified Quickmart
Kilimani, Kilimani, Nairobi. A separate public search for “bicycle” was checked
with Westlands, Nairobi selected on the storefront. Eighteen distinct
bicycle-related product pages were visible, although the page heading reported
19 results. The seller was independently checked for one BMX listing and
identified as Quickmart Westlands 24 HRS; the other 17 product pages were not
checked individually and remain labeled as a Westlands delivery-area context.
Items resolving to Ruaka, Kiambu were excluded rather than attributed to Nairobi.

No current, location-specific Nairobi prices were verified for Carrefour or
Jaza. At the time of this check, Naivas Online displayed a maintenance notice,
Carrefour pages timed out, and Jaza could not be reached. This snapshot is not
a complete list of retailers, product listings, or Nairobi branches.

The 2 kg sugar comparison is category-level only: Mumias and Kabras are
different brands. Missing retailers mean “no verified observation”, not
“unavailable”. The bicycle prices are public advertised listing prices, not
checkout totals or stock guarantees; Quickmart's terms say availability and
final price are confirmed at checkout.
